## Brand & Style

The design system embodies a modern, tactile, and sunlit hospitality aesthetic. It rejects sterile, high-tech tropes and cold enterprise dashboards in favor of an organic atmosphere that feels like natural linen, warm stoneware, and botanical depth. The UI balances two worlds: an unhurried, welcoming guest dining experience accessed via mobile QR codes, and a high-efficiency operational workstation for waitstaff, kitchen display systems (KDS), and restaurant managers.

The target audience spans restaurant patrons scanning menus on mobile connections in diverse ambient lighting, as well as floor staff, chefs, and restaurateurs interacting rapidly on tablets and desktop monitors during dinner rushes. The emotional response must feel calm, dependable, tactile, and effortlessly legible.

The aesthetic philosophy draws on **Tactile Warmth & Organic Modernism**:
- Deep forest greenery anchors brand authority and foundational interactions.
- Earthy terracotta provides purposeful, high-visibility human accents for urgent service requests and closing tables.
- Soft, generous surfaces, generous touch targets (minimum 44px up to 56px), and subtle micro-elevations deliver tactile security under rapid single-handed or greasy-screen operations.

## Colors

The color palette centers on warm, high-contrast, organic tones engineered for diverse lighting conditions (from candlelit dining rooms to bright commercial kitchens):

- **Warm Linen (`#FAF8F4`)**: The global light canvas. It eliminates the glaring harshness of pure `#FFFFFF` while mimicking organic menu paper.
- **Deep Forest Green (`#1E3F20`)**: The primary brand anchor. Used for primary CTAs ("Submit Order", "Add to Cart", "Sign In"), active navigation states, and persistent sticky cart capsules.
- **Terracotta Clay (`#C85A32`) & Sunlit Terracotta (`#D96B43`)**: The secondary and tertiary warm accents. Dedicated to time-critical hospitality alerts, the "Call Waiter" and "Request Bill" actions, and high-priority kitchen tickets.
- **Surface Porcelain (`#FFFFFF`)**: Used strictly for elevated cards, tickets, floating menus, and drawers.
- **Deep Pine Charcoal (`#1C241D`)**: Replaces pure black for headings and body text, preserving maximum contrast while maintaining natural color warmth.

### Operational State Tokens
The system uses strict semantic color pairs to avoid visual ambiguity during service:
- **Empty / Inactive**: `#E7E3D9` with `#525E53` text.
- **Ordered / New**: Slate Teal (`#2C6B7E` text on `#E0F0F4` container).
- **Waiting / Preparing**: Amber Ochre (`#C9821E` text on `#FCF0D8` container).
- **Bill Requested**: Terracotta Clay (`#C85A32` text on `#FCE6DD` container).
- **Ready / Delivered**: Herb Green (`#2E6F40` text on `#DFF0E3` container).
- **Error / Sold Out**: Crimson Brick (`#B9382C` text on `#FDE4E1` container).

## Typography

The typographical pairing unites **Plus Jakarta Sans** and **Inter**:
- **Plus Jakarta Sans** provides structural personality, rounded aperture warmth, and approachability across display headings, card titles, category pills, and buttons.
- **Inter** handles dense data layers, menu dish descriptions, notes, and manager table cells with utilitarian neutrality.

### Numerical Alignment & Optical Legibility
- All prices, ticket countdown timers, wait times, and dashboard analytics **must** employ `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`. This prevents numerical layout shift as timers tick or currencies update dynamically.
- `body-base` maintains an explicit `16px` minimum size on mobile screens to prevent automatic zoom behavior on iOS Safari when tapping form inputs and order notes.
- Letter spacing is tightened on large titles (`-0.02em`) to produce a tailored editorial feel, while labels and status chips use slight positive tracking (`+0.01em` to `+0.02em`) for clarity in quick visual scans.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an 8-point spatial rhythm (with 4px micro-increments), adapted for three key operational environments:

### Form Factor Breakpoints & Layout Models
1. **Mobile (360px – 639px | Guest Ordering & Mobile Waiter)**
   - **Model**: Single-column vertical stream with `1rem` outer margins and `0.75rem` to `1rem` element gaps.
   - **Chrome Consideration**: Safe-area bottom padding (`env(safe-area-inset-bottom)`) is strictly reserved for sticky cart bars, primary floating action bars, and order status summaries.
2. **Tablet (640px – 1023px | Waiter Station & KDS Landscape)**
   - **Waiter Grid**: 3- to 4-column responsive grid with `1.25rem` gutters alongside a collapsible 320px open requests sidebar.
   - **KDS View**: Locked 3-column Kanban layout (`New`, `Preparing`, `Ready`). Columns scroll independently so board headers, column counts, and column action targets never exit the viewport.
3. **Desktop (1024px+ | Manager Dashboard & Menu Editor)**
   - **Model**: Fixed `240px` navigation rail on `surface-oat`, fluid central workspace with `margin-desktop` (`2rem`), max-width constrained to `1280px` for readability.
   - **Editor Split**: Split-screen master-detail layout with an interactive `420px` slide-out configuration drawer for menu item customization.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses physical material metaphors rather than heavy drop shadows. Surfaces feel layered like pressed paper and smooth ceramic plates.

### Surface Tiers
- **Tier 0 (Backdrop / Canvas)**: `surface` Warm Linen (`#FAF8F4`) - flat, non-elevated ground.
- **Tier 1 (Resting Cards & KDS Tickets)**: `surface-porcelain` (`#FFFFFF`) with a `1px` structural outline (`#D8D4C8`) and a diffuse ambient shadow: `box-shadow: 0 2px 8px rgba(28, 36, 29, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Sticky Headers, Drawers & Modals)**: `surface-porcelain` (`#FFFFFF`) with `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px rgba(28, 36, 29, 0.08)` and a `1px` hairline border.
- **Floating Controls (Sticky Cart Pill)**: Elevated floating Forest Green pill with `box-shadow: 0 8px 20px rgba(30, 63, 32, 0.22)`.

### Border Rationale
Low-contrast `1px solid #D8D4C8` outlines are applied to all porcelain surfaces to maintain strong card separation under dim hospitality lighting or high-glare tablet screens.

## Shapes

The design system adheres to a rounded geometry (Value `2`), striking a friendly, approachable, yet organized balance.

### Shape Geometry Application
- **Standard Controls & Text Inputs**: `0.5rem` (8px) to `0.75rem` (12px) for structural predictability.
- **Cards, Order Tickets, and Modals**: `1rem` (16px) for standalone dish items, table blocks, and ticket containers, scaling up to `1.25rem` (20px) on tablet and desktop screens.
- **Interactive Badges, Category Chips & Primary Floating Action Bars**: Fully rounded `rounded-full` (`9999px`) pill shapes to signal tap affordance and contrast against rectangular content blocks.
- **Food Imagery**: `0.75rem` (12px) rounded corners, ensuring photographs feel integrated rather than floating as detached squares.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Touch Target**: Minimum height `44px` across mobile and desktop; critical service and kitchen triggers scale to `52px`–`56px`.
- **Primary Action**: Deep Forest Green (`#1E3F20`) background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-full` or `rounded-lg` (12px), active state scaling to `0.98`.
- **Urgent Service / Terracotta**: Terracotta Clay (`#C85A32`) fill, `#FFFFFF` text for guest "Call Waiter" and "Request Bill" CTAs.
- **Secondary / Ghost**: `#FFFFFF` background with `1.5px solid #D8D4C8` outline and Deep Pine text (`#1C241D`), transitioning to `#F5F2EB` on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- **Category Filter Chips**: `36px` to `40px` pill height. Inactive chips have `#EFECE4` fill and `#525E53` text; active chips feature `#1E3F20` fill with `#FFFFFF` text.
- **Table / Context Badge**: `28px` pill (`#DCE8DD` fill, `#132A14` text) displaying table markers (e.g., "Table 7") prominently in navigation headers.
- **Status Pills**: Compact `label-sm` badges using operational state tokens with high-contrast text and soft container fills (e.g., "Ready" uses `#2E6F40` on `#DFF0E3`).

### Form Inputs & Toggles
- **Input Fields**: Crisp `#FFFFFF` surface, `1.5px solid #D8D4C8` border, `12px` corner radius, `48px` minimum height, and `12px 16px` padding. The focus state engages a `#1E3F20` border with a soft `3px` ring in `#DCE8DD`.
- **Toggle Switches (Menu Availability)**: `48x28px` pill track. Active (available) uses `#2E6F40`; inactive/sold-out shifts to `#D8D4C8`.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Guest Menu Item Card**: Horizontal layout on mobile featuring an 88x88px rounded photo, dish title, 2-line truncated description, tabular price, and a `44x44px` Forest Green `+` add button (morphing into a quantity stepper when selected).
- **Sticky Cart Bar**: Floating pill capsule docked `16px` above the bottom safe area. Styled in `#1E3F20` with white text, showing item badge count, price sum, and "View Cart" indicator.
- **Waiter Table Tile**: Elevated card with table designation, seat capacity, active duration, and a prominent 4px status accent bar along the top matching the table's state (`Empty`, `Ordered`, `Waiting`, `Bill Requested`).
- **KDS Ticket Card**: Grouped by order/table with tabular timer badges (shifting to `#C9821E` when overdue), special instructions highlighted in `#FCF0D8`, and a full-width `52px` status advancement button ("Start Preparing" / "Mark Ready").
